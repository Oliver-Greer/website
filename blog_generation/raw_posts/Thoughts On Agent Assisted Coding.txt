Supposedly 2026 is the year of the agent, and indeed agents are taking over our lives. 
Just about everyone I know is using agents to write their code, 
and sometimes even to review it. We are using agents to send emails, write technical documents, 
research, and even file our taxes.

This post won't address whether LLMs are good at this sort of thing.
There are lots of people much more qualified than I am that can write about that.
This post will instead address what I see as the greatest issue with LLM assisted coding,
especially given a world where the agents are effective enough to complete the vast majority of programming tasks.
